# APOGEE: Lunar Mission

APOGEE: Lunar Mission is a browser-based rocket building and space agency survival game created by **Team Peace** for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (Space Mission Design Game challenge).

In this game, you build multi-stage rockets, check real-time flight data, unlock new tech, and manage your budget to reach the Moon without going bankrupt.

## Main Features
-Rocket Workshop: Customize Stage 1 and Stage 2 parts, fuel levels, payload weight, booster recovery kits, and launch insurance.                                                                                         
-Live Flight Data: Check lift-off weight, thrust-to-weight ratio (TWR), total Delta-V, and safety ratings before launching.                                                                                             
-6 Lunar Milestones: Start from simple test flights and work your way up to deep-space Moon missions while taking on commercial jobs.                                                                                    
-Budget and R&D: Earn science points to unlock better engines, collect patent income, and survive monthly running costs.                                                                                                 
-Easy to Play Anywhere: Runs smoothly in any web browser with auto-save and supports both English and Traditional Chinese.

## NASA Data and Science
The game uses real rocket physics and mission ideas inspired by NASA's Apollo and Artemis Moon programs. This includes fuel-to-weight math, engine efficiency, and the speed (Delta-V) needed to reach Earth orbit and travel to the Moon.
